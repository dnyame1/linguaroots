# LinguaRoots 

A heritage language learning iOS app built for the Ghanaian diaspora — 
starting with Twi. Designed for people reconnecting with a language their 
family already speaks, where the gap is generational, not academic.

Most language apps are built for tourists. LinguaRoots is built for something 
different.

---

## What It Does

### Onboarding
New users complete a welcome survey that personalizes their learning path based 
on their connection to the language — whether they grew up hearing it at home, 
learned a few words from family, or are starting from scratch.

### Interactive Units
4 structured units — Greetings, Family, Everyday, Food — built around how 
language is actually passed down through rhythm, repetition, and oral tradition 
rather than static flashcard drilling.

### Quizzes
Each unit ends with a quiz to reinforce what was learned and track comprehension 
over time.

### Streak & Gamification System
XP tracking, level progression, daily streaks, and an in-app shop reward 
consistent practice. The more you engage, the more you unlock.

### AI Conversation Feature
An embedded AI conversation feature lets users practice real Twi dialogue — 
moving beyond passive vocabulary into active use.

## Why Twi

Twi is spoken by the Akan people of Ghana and is one of the most widely spoken 
languages in West Africa. For many in the diaspora, it's the language of 
grandparents, of phone calls home, of things said at the dinner table that 
never quite got translated. LinguaRoots exists to close that gap.
